Bench EndPoint measures the transmission/reception speed (bandwidth) of local network between the PC or smartphone connected to the router. Bench EndPoint is running with Windows ipTIME Bench v1.04 or later. ipTIME Bench for Windows can be downloaded from ipTIME homepage. (homepage: www.iptime.com)
